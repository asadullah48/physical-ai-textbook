# Introduction to Physical AI

## Define Physical AI
Physical AI (PAI) distinguishes itself from virtual AI by its embodiment. It refers to AI systems that perceive, reason, and act in the physical world. Unlike Generative AI (like ChatGPT) which outputs text or images, a Physical AI agent outputs motor commands, torques, and forces to manipulate the environment.

## Key Components
1. **Perception**: Using sensors (cameras, LiDAR, IMU) to understand the state of the world.
2. **Reasoning**: Planning actions to achieve a goal (path planning, task planning).
3. **Actuation**: Controlling motors to execute the plan.
4. **Embodiment**: The physical form factor (robot arm, humanoid, drone) constraints what the AI can do.

## Why now?
The convergence of Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), Simulation (Sim-to-Real), and cheap powerful hardware (GPUs, motors) has led to a boom in Physical AI.
